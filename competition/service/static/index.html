Agentic Visual Intelligence for Produce Inspection

OpenCV 5 evaluates whether the visual evidence is trustworthy, takes bounded corrective actions when appropriate, and runs the condition model only when the capture is suitable.

Use one primary fruit per image. Apple, banana or orange. Market stalls, piles and trees with many fruits are outside the current scope and will be sent back for a recapture.

1 · Submit a capture

Drop an image, or choose a file

JPG or PNG · up to 12 MB · one primary fruit

PNG is lossless, so its pixels reach the agent exactly as encoded. JPEG is lossy and can shift a marginal capture across a threshold.

    Responsible use
    
    
    AgriVision assesses visible produce condition from
      images. It does not detect pathogens, toxins, microbiological
      contamination or internal spoilage, and it does not determine whether food
      is safe to eat. A person remains responsible for any decision about
      produce.
